Game setup

  • Use small-game cards, questions, and values.
  • Two players hide together as a pair; every other player seeks together as one team.
  • The hiding pair shares one station, hiding spot, answer, timer, and hider hand. Both members must be found to end the round.
  • The first round starts at Union Station's main concourse.
  • Later rounds start from the previous hiding pair's spot after the official 10-minute preparation period. Do not reset to Union Station.
  • The hiding period is 40 minutes. Not every mapped station needs to be reachable in every round.
  • Hiding zones are 400 m / 1/4 mile straight-line circles.
  • Only blue station markers can center a hiding zone.
  • Named map neighborhoods and cities are the divisions used for prefecture/canton questions.

Transportation

  • Metro Rail, Metro Bus, other scheduled public buses, Metrolink, and walking are valid.
  • Rideshare, taxis, private cars, bicycles, scooters, and bike share are not valid.
  • Players may ride through the area outside the red border, but may not leave transit there or use that trip to answer a location question.
  • A bus stop is not an eligible hiding-zone center unless marked blue on this map.

Google Maps only

  • Use Google Maps only for directions, distances, nearby-place searches, categories, geographic labels, screenshots, and live location sharing.
  • The supplied interactive game map is the only exception; use it for the game border, eligible stations, divisions, and hiding zones.
  • Do not use other mapping or transit-planning apps to answer questions or make game decisions.
  • Google Street View and similar street-level imagery remain prohibited.

Stations and end game

  • A multi-line station complex counts as one station. Use every line shown in its popup.
  • "Nearest station" means the nearest eligible blue marker by straight-line distance.
  • Seekers are on Metro Rail until they exit the paid station area. The end game begins when they exit inside the hider's zone.
  • Waiting at a bus stop is not being on transit.

Safety

  • The final spot must be publicly accessible, within 10 feet of a mapped path or road, and safe for every player to approach.
  • Pause immediately for unsafe heat, smoke, police activity, medical issues, service disruptions, or any player feeling unsafe.
  • Excluded areas include freeway and rail infrastructure, flood channels, construction and industrial sites, school grounds, private residences, encampments, isolated trails, and anywhere closed or requiring trespass.
  • Any area a player identifies as uncomfortable becomes excluded immediately without debate or strategic penalty.
