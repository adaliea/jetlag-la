# Jet Lag: Hide + Seek Los Angeles

## LA Standard Rules

These are local rulings for use with the official Hide + Seek home-game cards
and rules. If this document does not address a situation, use the
[official rules](https://jetlag.denull.ru/en/rules/).

### Game setup

| Setting | LA ruling |
| --- | --- |
| Game size | **Small**; use all small-game card values and questions |
| Team format | Each round, **two players hide together as a pair**; every other player forms **one seeker team** and seeks together |
| Expected duration | 4-8 hours for one round per player |
| First-round starting point | **Union Station**, in the main concourse |
| Later-round starting point | The previous hiding pair's hiding spot |
| Hiding period | **40 minutes** |
| Hiding-zone radius | **400 m / 1/4 mile**, straight-line distance |
| Eligible zone centers | Blue station markers on the supplied map |
| Valid seeker transportation | Metro Rail, Metro Bus, other scheduled public buses, Metrolink, and walking |
| Valid hider transportation during hiding/Move | Same as seekers, but the new zone center must be an eligible blue station |
| Divisions for prefecture/canton questions | Named neighborhood or city/CDP polygons shown on the map |
| Required map app | **Google Maps only** |

Use Metro's current service information on game day. A station that is
temporarily closed remains an eligible zone center only if the hider can
legally and safely reach its mapped public entrance during the hiding period.
Planned stations that have not opened are never eligible.

The official small-game hiding period is 30 minutes, but this LA map uses
**40 minutes** because of the network's geographic spread, transfers, and
headways. Not every mapped station needs to be reachable in every round.

### Teams and rotation

- The hider is always a pair of two players. The pair travels, chooses one
  station and hiding spot, answers questions, manages one shared hider hand,
  and is found together.
- Every player who is not in the hiding pair forms one seeker team. Seekers
  must travel and make decisions together.
- Set the hiding-pair rotation before the game so every player gets a fair
  number of hiding rounds.
- A round ends when the seeker team finds both members of the hiding pair.
- The first round starts at Union Station. After a pair is found, everyone
  remains at that hiding spot.
- The next hiding pair gets the official **10-minute preparation period**.
  After preparation, the next 40-minute hiding period starts from the previous
  pair's hiding spot. Do not reset to Union Station between rounds.

### Game area

The game area is the red polygon on the supplied map. It covers the
rail-connected core from the Westside through Downtown and Northeast LA, plus
the North Hollywood branch.

- A hiding zone is legal only when its eligible station center is shown on the
  map. The mapped station list has already excluded centers whose full 400 m
  circle would materially cross the border.
- Hiders and seekers may briefly ride through the area outside the border,
  but may not leave transit there or use that trip to answer a location
  question.
- Seekers may use any in-area stop for travel. Only blue rail-station markers
  can center a hiding zone.

### Station rulings

- A station complex with multiple lines is **one station**. This includes
  Union Station, 7th Street / Metro Center, Expo / Crenshaw, and
  Wilshire / Vermont.
- For questions about the hider's station, use every Metro Rail line listed in
  that station's map popup.
- Entrances, platforms, and bus bays belonging to one complex do not count as
  separate stations.
- "Nearest station" means nearest eligible blue station by straight-line
  distance, not travel time.
- A station name is the current name shown in Metro's GTFS feed. Ignore old
  names visible on signs or third-party maps.

### Neighborhood and matching rulings

- For prefecture/canton questions, use the polygon containing the eligible
  station center, not the hider's exact hiding spot.
- City/CDP polygons outside the City of Los Angeles and named LA
  neighborhoods inside it are equivalent divisions.
- If a station center lies exactly on a boundary, use the division containing
  the majority of its 400 m hiding zone. Settle this before seekers ask the
  question.
- For all category questions, follow the official Google Maps legitimacy
  rules. Seekers should send a Google Maps screenshot when a category is
  ambiguous.

### Google Maps only

- Use **Google Maps only** for transit directions, walking directions,
  distances, nearby-place searches, category questions, geographic labels,
  screenshots, and live location sharing.
- Do not use Apple Maps, OpenStreetMap, Transit, Citymapper, Metro's trip
  planner, or another mapping app to answer questions or make game decisions.
- The supplied interactive LA game map is the only exception. Use it to
  determine the game border, eligible blue stations, divisions, and 400 m
  hiding zones.
- Google Street View and similar street-level imagery remain prohibited under
  the official rules.
- Metro alerts and official timetables may be checked only to confirm service
  disruptions or whether transit is operating, not to replace Google Maps
  directions during normal play.

### LA transit edge cases

- Metro Rail is valid from entering the paid station area until exiting it.
  The end game begins when seekers exit a station inside the hider's zone,
  not while their train passes through it.
- A bus counts as transit from boarding until stepping off. Waiting at a stop
  is not being on transit.
- Rideshare, taxis, private cars, bicycles, scooters, and bike share are not
  valid transportation.
- Free station shuttles count only when they are publicly available,
  scheduled or continuously circulating, and shown by an official operator.
- Service disruptions are force majeure. Pause all timers while the group
  resolves a disruption that makes continued play unsafe or impossible.

### Safety rules

- The final hiding spot must be publicly accessible, within 10 feet of a
  mapped path or road, and safe for every player to approach.
- Pause immediately for unsafe heat, wildfire smoke, police activity, medical
  issues, or a player feeling unsafe. Safety pauses confer no strategic
  advantage.
- Carry water, a charged phone, a TAP card, and a battery pack.

### Excluded areas

The following areas are outside the game even when they fall inside the red
map border or a legal 400 m hiding zone:

- Freeways, freeway shoulders, ramps, medians, and land enclosed
  by freeway ramps.
- Rail tracks, tunnels, yards, maintenance facilities, rights-of-way, and any
  platform or paid area entered without a valid fare.
- Active construction sites, demolition sites, utility facilities, industrial
  yards, loading docks, and parking structures signed against public access.
- Hospitals and medical facilities beyond public-facing lobbies, cafes, and
  outdoor public paths.
- Encampments and occupied living spaces. Do not enter, photograph, or use
  them as hiding landmarks.
- Any place that is closed, fenced, gated, signed private, requires trespass,
  requires climbing, or where staff/security ask a player to leave.
- Any block, station, park, or path that a player identifies before or during
  the game as uncomfortable. This exclusion takes effect immediately without
  debate or strategic penalty.

These exclusions restrict where players may walk and hide; they do not remove
an otherwise eligible blue station as a zone center. If an exclusion makes a
zone impractical, the hider must choose another station.

## Recommended variants

### Faster evening game

Use the standard map, a 30-minute hiding period, and a hard three-hour round
cap. Do not begin a new round less than three hours before the last useful
rail service.

### Bus-expanded expert game

After testing the standard game, agree on a short, fixed list of major bus
transit centers before play. Do not treat every multi-line street stop as a
zone center; that produced 581 centers on the older map and substantially
changes small-game balance.

Suggested candidates to evaluate as a group include Harbor Gateway Transit
Center, Westwood/UCLA, and Glendale Transportation Center. Add a candidate
only if its full 400 m zone is inside the agreed border and everyone is
comfortable hiding there.

## Pre-game checklist

- Confirm the map's data date and check Metro alerts.
- Confirm that every player has Google Maps and that Google Maps live location
  sharing works.
- Confirm every player is comfortable with all parts of the map.
- Randomize the hiding-pair order and confirm Union Station as the first-round
  meeting point.
- Confirm small-game cards/values, 40-minute hiding period, and 400 m zones.
- Set an end time and a rule for unfinished final rounds.
